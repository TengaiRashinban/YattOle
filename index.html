Your own AI. Free, and it grows with you.

YattOle is the Other Me — a personal, agentic AI that lives on your iPhone and Mac, learns your day, and handles what you hand it. No subscription, no account to pay for. Everything stays inside what you own.

Coming soon to the App Store · United States

Requires an iPhone or Mac with Apple Intelligence (iOS 27 / macOS 27). A Mac is optional — it makes the Other Me stronger.

How it works

Three rungs, all yours

Every question starts on the device in your hand and climbs only as far as it needs to. Warm colors are yours alone — blue means Apple's private servers stepped in, and even then, they can't read it.

ON THIS IPHONE

Fast, everyday answers

Free, on-device answers powered by Apple Intelligence. This is where the Other Me learns your day — your schedule, your people, your promises.

ON YOUR MAC

A bigger brain for harder questions

When a question needs more thought, your Mac quietly takes over with a 27B-class open-weight model — still free, kept entirely inside your own iCloud.

APPLE PCC · OPT-IN

A pinch hitter that can't peek

For the toughest questions, Apple's Private Cloud Compute can help too — verifiably unable to read what you send, and only if you allow it.

Privacy

Private by architecture, not by promise

0
Requests to third-party AI

Structurally zero — there is no code path that sends your content to any AI service outside Apple's. Conversations, memories, and minutes live on your devices and in your own iCloud private database. The developer has no servers and no access — technically none, not merely by policy.

Meetings, transcribed on-device

Live minutes with action items. Audio is never recorded, saved, or uploaded.

Promises that come back

Hand something off and the Other Me surfaces it when it matters — follow-ups, not forgetting.

Your people, remembered

Who you met, when you last talked, what you promised — kept for you, not about you.

Your day, briefed

Weather, schedule, and a one-line morning brief — read from your real calendar, never guessed.
